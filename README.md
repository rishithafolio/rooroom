# ✦ ROOROOM ✦
### *One room. Two moods.*

**Born on 11.11.2026**

*A little room. A little chaos. A lot of comfort.*

---

**ROOROOM** is a tiny interactive room experience where temperature changes the atmosphere, and comfort takes centre stage.

Switch between cooling and heating, watch the room transform, and let **Roo** enjoy her little corner of the world.

> 💻 **Best Experienced on Desktop:** `v1.0.0` is designed for desktop screens to showcase the complete interactive room layout. **Mobile support is coming in `v1.1.0`!**

---

## ❄️ FEATURES

### ❄️ AC Mode
Cool blue atmosphere with animated airflow.

### 🔥 Heater Mode
Warm atmosphere with glowing heat effects.

### ⚡ Power Off
A sudden blackout that switches off the appliances.

### 🛋️ Dynamic Comfort
Roo's blanket responds to the selected temperature.

### ✨ Interactive Environment
An illustrated room with independently animated elements.

## 📖 THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

**ROOROOM** is a playful combination of **ROO + ROOM**.

The name brings together Roo and the space at the heart of this experience — a room that changes its atmosphere with every temperature setting.

A simple play on words became the identity of the project.

**ROOROOM. A name for a little world of its own.**
